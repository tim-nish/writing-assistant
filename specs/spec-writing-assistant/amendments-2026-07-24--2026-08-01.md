> **Amended 2026-07-30 (triage, #957) — the FULL tier gains a fourth remedy, PARALLEL EXECUTION, and its licence is a per-check declaration that defaults to unsafe.** The clause above deferred restructuring-how-checks-execute to its own evidence; that evidence is now taken and it discharges the deferral rather than relaxing it.
