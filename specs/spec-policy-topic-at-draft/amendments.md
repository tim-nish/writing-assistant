> **Amended 2026-08-01 (triage, #1144; coupled with #1140/#1142) — for a BRIEF-CARRYING run the policy-topic set is DERIVED from the brief's members and rendered as a DISCLOSURE; the human gate survives only for a cold run, and either way the surface enters the gate registry.** CAP-2's two-step topic selection (`SPEC.md:38-50`) dates from #230, **before the Brief existed** and before the Strand/thread-line vocabulary was ratified. On 2026-08-01 it fired inside a run whose brief already carried five selected Strands with their pins, and asked the owner to choose policy topics for material the brief had already fixed. The owner's analysis, carried in substance: once the members are in the brief, the related topics are reverse-resolvable from them, and **no reason is stated anywhere for why the interview's seed material must be Topics** rather than the Strands the owner selected. **Two grounds, and only one of them is served.** The boundary test is covered — *"would changing this fact change the ARGUMENT, or only how the argument is RENDERED?"* (owner decision record — 2026-07-31 (article design boundary)) — and a topic selection that seeds which recorded positions may raise a tension is argument-side, so it belongs at the Brief or is derived from it, never re-asked at realization. The **Topics-vs-Strands** half is **uncovered**: `policy_lookup` returned no served position discriminating whether the interview's seed material must be Topics, and this amendment records that miss rather than implying a position. So the derivation is this consumer's decision, taken on the boundary test alone. **What is decided:** for a run carrying a brief, the topic set is derived from the members (Strand → lesson → served topic), rendered to the owner as a **disclosure naming which topics were derived and from which members**, and the ≤2 cap and the code-enforced whitelist are **unchanged** — a derivation may not widen what a read may reach. For a run with no brief the two-step gate stands exactly as CAP-2 states it. **The registry half binds both branches:** the surface has no entry in `draft_gates.GATES`, so it sits outside the payload audit and the pending-decision map that #1132 and story 20.117 built — an owner screen outside the registry contradicts *a run's gates are auditable*. It gains an entry whichever branch it takes; a disclosure that is not a gate is declared with `owner_decision: None`, which is the shape `resume-confirmation` already uses. **Overturn condition, stated because the uncovered half is real:** a cold run in which the derived set would have been wrong — a brief whose members' topics do not cover the tension worth raising. That is the observation this decision waits on, and it would reopen the derivation rather than the disclosure. Delivery is story 20.127, which carries this beside the interview changes because both land in `scripts/draft-pipeline.py` and `skills/draft-article/stages/stage2.md`.
