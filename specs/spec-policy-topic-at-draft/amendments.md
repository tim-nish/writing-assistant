> **Amended 2026-08-01 (triage, #1144; coupled with #1140/#1142) — for a BRIEF-CARRYING run the policy-topic set is DERIVED from the brief's members and rendered as a DISCLOSURE; the human gate survives only for a cold run, and either way the surface enters the gate registry.** CAP-2's two-step topic selection (`SPEC.md:38-50`) dates from #230, **before the Brief existed** and before the Strand/thread-line vocabulary was ratified. On 2026-08-01 it fired inside a run whose brief already carried five selected Strands with their pins, and asked the owner to choose policy topics for material the brief had already fixed. The owner's analysis, carried in substance: once the members are in the brief, the related topics are reverse-resolvable from them, and **no reason is stated anywhere for why the interview's seed material must be Topics** rather than the Strands the owner selected. **Two grounds, and only one of them is served.** The boundary test is covered — *"would changing this fact change the ARGUMENT, or only how the argument is RENDERED?"* (owner decision record — 2026-07-31 (article design boundary)) — and a topic selection that seeds which recorded positions may raise a tension is argument-side, so it belongs at the Brief or is derived from it, never re-asked at realization. The **Topics-vs-Strands** half is **uncovered**: `policy_lookup` returned no served position discriminating whether the interview's seed material must be Topics, and this amendment records that miss rather than implying a position. So the derivation is this consumer's decision, taken on the boundary test alone. **What is decided:** for a run carrying a brief, the topic set is derived from the members (Strand → lesson → served topic), rendered to the owner as a **disclosure naming which topics were derived and from which members**, and the ≤2 cap and the code-enforced whitelist are **unchanged** — a derivation may not widen what a read may reach. For a run with no brief the two-step gate stands exactly as CAP-2 states it. **The registry half binds both branches:** the surface has no entry in `draft_gates.GATES`, so it sits outside the payload audit and the pending-decision map that #1132 and story 20.117 built — an owner screen outside the registry contradicts *a run's gates are auditable*. It gains an entry whichever branch it takes; a disclosure that is not a gate is declared with `owner_decision: None`, which is the shape `resume-confirmation` already uses. **Overturn condition, stated because the uncovered half is real:** a cold run in which the derived set would have been wrong — a brief whose members' topics do not cover the tension worth raising. That is the observation this decision waits on, and it would reopen the derivation rather than the disclosure. Delivery is story 20.127, which carries this beside the interview changes because both land in `scripts/draft-pipeline.py` and `skills/draft-article/stages/stage2.md`.

Delivery is story 20.132, which carries only the code-comment correction this retraction falsifies (`scripts/draft_gates.py:205-213`, which currently asserts the amendment above ratifies a second branch).

> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1246 — OWNER RULING; FIRES THE #1168 REOPEN TRIGGER) — the policy-topic ask RETIRES, and the bound it was performing is replaced by the CLAIM rather than by another pre-pick: tension questions are composed from the thesis and its member Strands, and where one needs served policy it is asked as a QUERY.** The amendment above kept CAP-2's two-step gate for every run and recorded, deliberately undecided, that *"the original 2026-08-01 complaint — that a brief-carrying run asks for policy topics over material the brief already fixed — survives unanswered"*, with the **reopen trigger** *"an owner statement that Strand-seeded tension questions are the intended design."* That statement has been made. Owner, 2026-08-02, verbatim: *"The question 'Which policy topics seed this article?' should no longer exist. Confirm this. The ThesisStrands type does not require the user to select a Topic."* This is the reopen, taken as its own sitting per that trigger's own instruction.
>
> **The evidence that the question carries no owner-only information, from today's run.** `20260802T142948-588398` was brief-carrying — an adopted thesis over three Strands, all arcs served — and opened the interview by asking the owner to pick ≤2 policy topics. The recommendation engine then **derived the answer it was asking for**, identifying the only two topics holding the selected Strands' material, in the same breath as the ask. A question whose answer the asker computes is not a gate.
>
> **What retires, precisely.** `draft_gates.GATES["policy-topics"]` (`scripts/draft_gates.py:266-270`, `owner_decision: "which ≤2 policy topics this article's tension questions may be seeded from"`) retires — and CAP-2's two-step is **replaced rather than half-removed**, because removing the ask while keeping the pre-pick would only move the same decision off-screen. Seeding derives from the adopted thesis and its member Strands. The 2026-07-28 approach-per-axis ruling already points here — *"topic selection lives only inside the Decision flow"* — and a brief's population is not that flow.
>
> **The bound derives from the same place the seeding does, and that is the whole decision.** CAP-2's ≤2-file cap and the code-enforced whitelist were a **cost** bound over a pre-declared universe. It is replaced by a bound derived from the claim: each tension question needing served policy is asked as a query against the granted surface — claim in, matched lines out — with no topic files chosen in advance by anyone. **This is the same direction ratified the same day for repository reading** (`specs/spec-writing-assistant/amendments.md`, the 2026-08-02 #1182 amendment): *"read a corpus to test a stated claim, never to gather against an unstated one"*, whose upstream position is recorded at *(owner decision record — 2026-08-01 (retrieval direction: thesis-first examination))*. The ≤2-file cap is the policy-surface twin of the declared-source denominator that amendment dropped, and dropping both is one direction applied to both corpora rather than two coincident decisions. The Constraints section's *"per-run selection widens which two topic files, never how many or what else is readable"* is superseded for brief-carrying runs by a claim-scoped read; the **permission** boundary — which surface may be consulted at all — is untouched, exactly as the source declaration survived as a permission boundary when it stopped being a coverage denominator.
>
> **Cold (brief-less) runs: the ask does not return, and this is decided rather than left open.** #1246 puts the cold case in scope and the answer follows from the bound above: a cold run has no thesis, therefore no claim, therefore nothing to bound a policy read with — so it reads nothing policy-side until a thesis exists. Reinstating the ask there would keep alive, on the thinnest population, the one question this amendment retires.
>
> **The premise this decision does NOT rest on, stated because the amendment above measured it false.** #1168 retracted a `Strand → lesson → served topic` derivation on evidence: against a real five-member brief every member's served `topic` was the synthetic bucket `hub-lessons` and the intersection with the nine served topic files was **empty**. Nothing here rebuilds that join. The seeding described above is a **query against the served surface**, not a lookup through a topic-file mapping, and the alternative that would have re-ratified the join — machine-derives the same ≤2 topics and discloses them — was weighed and declined at this gate for exactly that reason.
>
> **Acceptance is a replayed run, not a code-path grep**, per #1246's own constraint and the class's history (#1144 → #1152 → #1168 is three removals that were superseded, retracted, or landed in a path the runs do not take): a brief-carrying run in which the interview opens with no topic ask. **What would overturn this amendment,** and it is a measured risk rather than a hypothetical: the query transport serves only query-**matched** lines and cannot enumerate topics or serve the glossary whole, so a seeding step that needs breadth rather than answers degrades to generic. If tension-question composition proves to need the corpus rather than an answer, this is wrong and the answer becomes the deliberately-widened read — glossary, lessons, and every topic the thesis's Strands touch, disclosed per run rather than capped in advance — which was the third alternative at this gate. Delivery: stories 20.160 (gate retirement and claim-bounded seeding), 20.161 (reader path and config keys).

> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1256 — a CORRECTION to the amendment above, and one OPEN question it must not decide alone).** Delivery of story 20.161 stopped and reported the story misclassified, on evidence verified at implementation time. Both survivals are **ratified**: SPEC-terrain amended 2026-07-29 (#886) — *"The `≤2 topics/*.md` bound survives unchanged for anything that still reads a raw thread"* — and amended 2026-07-28 (#873) — *"`track_topics` keeps its track↔topic mapping role and stops being an axis denominator."* The #1246 sitting cited neither, because it never looked outside the draft interview.
>
> **A second premise was already false when written.** CAP-3's `track`/`topics` keys and the `set-policy-source --track/--topics` writer flags were removed at Story 13.36 and #525; `SPEC-policy-source-seam` carries them struck through, and `SPEC-repo-onboarding` CAP-2's 2026-07-21 amendment states that the `track_topics` mapping writer *"**replaces** the removed `--track/--topics` value flags … rather than reviving them."* The triage that wrote story 20.161 read CAP-3's prose as pending work; the code had moved a month earlier. And CAP-3's own removal precondition — *"once no owned repo config carries them"* — is **unmet** for `track_topics`: a sweep of 201 resolved config files found one live carrier, the owner's own host repo, read on every terrain run.
>
> **What this amendment therefore corrects:** the retirement above binds **the draft interview's topic pick** — the gate, its two-step, and the ≤2 pre-declared coverage bound as the *interview's* seeding mechanism. It does **not** reach `read --topics` as a reader input, and it does **not** reach `track_topics` as terrain's track↔topic mapping. Story 20.160's delivery is unaffected and stands; nothing in it removed either.
>
> **What is left OPEN, deliberately, because a story cannot resolve a conflict between two ratified specs:** does the #1246 retirement reach SPEC-terrain's degraded element read and its `track_topics` denominator, or does #886's preservation stand? That is a spec question with a concrete cost — a removal would break the owner's live host-repo mapping — and it is routed back to the spec lane rather than answered here. **Until it is answered, `read --topics`, `track_topics`, `validate-config.py`'s existence lint and `check-config-validation.sh` sections 8a–8d all STAY.**
