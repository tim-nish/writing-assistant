> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1220/#1223) — the retirement boundary is the SHIPPED STAGE ORDER, not the word: CAP-1 and CAP-10 are rewritten over `examine`, and every remaining `harvest`/`fact sheet` occurrence is declared VOCABULARY RESIDUE, report-only, with the owner-reaching surface guarded by a check.** #1220 named six residual surfaces and #1223 named seven more outside them, proposing that #1220's list be widened. **Measured at triage rather than estimated, which is what killed the enumeration framing:** `harvest`/`fact sheet` appears **918 times** repo-wide (specs 137, skills 128, scripts 566, docs 76, README 11), across **26 spec files**, and inside **7 of this spec's 10 capabilities** (CAP-1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10). Both lists are therefore far short of the population, and a list already widened once and proposed for widening again is precisely the tell the served architecture line names — *"an enumerated prohibition can only name yesterday's leak while a construction constraint makes tomorrow's unreachable … the checkable tell that you are on the wrong side is a check suite growing at roughly one member per incident"* (*(owner decision record — 2026-07-28 (constrain generation rather than improve post-hoc detection))*). Exhausting a 918-member enumeration is that shape at its largest. **What is retired is therefore the FALSEHOOD, not the token.** The two capabilities *defined over* harvest are rewritten to state what ships: CAP-1 becomes per-claim grounding at the read that produced it (no pre-extraction, no standing fact sheet), and CAP-10's determinism contract is re-based from the whole-corpus pass onto per-claim examination, keeping all three of its mechanisms and dropping the coverage manifest the same day's #1224 amendment removes. **The residue is declared, with its boundary stated so the declaration is not read as coverage:** the remaining occurrences are report-only, they are *not* asserted to be harmless, and nothing here promises they will be worked to zero. What binds instead is one property — **no owner-reaching surface carries harvest vocabulary** — because that is the subset with a reader, and it is the subset #1223 correctly identified as highest-value (its item 1, the `harvest-completion` registry entry, carries `owner_decision: "continue into drafting, or stop with the fact sheet kept"` at `scripts/draft_gates.py:229-239` and surfaced to the owner verbatim in the 2026-08-02 run — read at triage, with its emitter at `:514` and the registry-order assertion at `scripts/check-gate-inventory.sh:92-93`). **What would overturn this:** "owner-reaching" proving unclassifiable by a mechanical check, in which case the guard is unbuildable and the honest fallback is the owner-reaching stories alone with the spec left false — the option this decision rejected, and the one it degrades to rather than pretending otherwise. **#1223 is not absorbed into #1220 and neither closes on this amendment**; each carries its own stories. > **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1248) — the fill's machine-paced work FANS OUT, behind two writer contracts and one corrected attestation contract that are defects in their own right TODAY: the pin ledger becomes DERIVED, examination records become ID-NAMED, and sharded provenance verdicts arrive as REPEATED FILES with concatenation REFUSED.** Owner-paced gates are out of scope — their duration is owner attention and no parallelism buys it back — and probe is out of scope at its ≤5s ceiling. What remains serial and need not be: per-claim examinations, the three sources inside one examination, the provenance judging, and the visual-set work.
>
> **The two writer contracts are prerequisites, not options, and each is a live defect without any concurrency.** **(1) The pin ledger is not concurrency-safe and stops being written concurrently at all.** `examine.py:393-410` dedupes by reading `examination-pins.txt` into a set and then appending — two concurrent examinations can both pass the read, interleave their appends, and produce duplicate or torn lines. The fix preserves determinism rather than adding a lock: each examination writes **only its own record**, which it already does, and the ledger is **derived at the join** from the examination records **in claim order** — so the declared pointer set `verify-provenance` resolves against is byte-identical regardless of completion order, and the only shared write between concurrent examinations is removed rather than guarded. The acceptance is *the same pins from a serial and a concurrent run*, never absence-of-crash. **(2) Examination record names become claim ids.** `examine.py:400-401` names the record from the claim slugged and truncated to 60 characters, so two long claims sharing a prefix **silently overwrite each other** — a defect in purely serial runs today, which parallelism makes urgent rather than causes. Pre-enumerated claim ids from the fan-out list close it.
>
> **(3) The sharded-attestation contract, CORRECTED against #1248's own text, and the correction is why this clause is stated rather than delegated.** #1248 offers *"multiple verdicts files (or a concatenation)"*. Verified at triage: `verify-provenance.py:228-231` sets `draft_hash` **last-wins** across the parsed text, so a concatenation whose final shard is fresh would **silently accept a stale earlier shard** — precisely the fail-open the issue's next bullet forbids (*"a shard hash mismatch fails the whole gate … a stale shard is 'not judged,' never dropped"*). The contract is therefore: the verdicts input is **repeatable**, each file opens with its own attestation binding the **same** `draft-sha256` and its own `graded:` line, coverage is checked over the **union**, **any** hash disagreement fails the whole gate, and **concatenation is refused rather than supported**. **Correction, recorded rather than silently fixed (delivery, story 20.163):** this clause was drafted naming the flag `--verdicts`, and **no such flag existed** — the shipped input is `--judge-findings`. The delivery added `--verdicts` as an *alias on the same dest* rather than renaming, so no caller breaks and both spellings are repeatable. The defect is this amendment's own: a contract naming an interface it never read is the same class of unverified binding the grounding rule forbids in acceptance criteria, committed here in spec text. It still fails closed when the union does not cover every narration/derived position. Already existing and reused rather than rebuilt: `graded:` lines are unioned today (`verify-provenance.py:218-241`), and the echo / `ANCHOR MISMATCH` machinery is per-position and needs no change.
>
> **The four fan-outs, once the contracts hold.** Examinations of distinct claims share nothing, so once the argument plan and section intents exist the claims needing repository grounding are enumerable up front and their examinations run concurrently, with the records consumed as sections are written; claims emerging mid-fill stay inline. **This is not a stockpile regression and the amendment says so explicitly against the owner's 2026-08-02 reading-pattern ruling:** every read is still triggered by a stated claim that exists before the read — only the *scheduling* changes, never the trigger — and widening enumeration to "claims we might need" is what must not happen. Inside one examination, the three sources (declared prose, anchored commits, issues, each a 60s-ceilinged `run()`) go concurrent: the issues source is network-bound, the three share no state, their per-source `searched`/`skipped` records are already kept apart, and the output contract is preserved byte-for-byte. The provenance judge shards across isolated subagents under contract (3), each shard as isolated from the drafting context as the single judge is today. The visual-set plan and proposals run beside the judging — they depend on the draft and the argument plan, the judging on neither, and the quality gate is where both are already required. **Correction, recorded rather than silently fixed (delivery, story 20.164):** this clause was drafted asserting that *"the quality gate already consumes both"*, and it does not. `quality-gate` takes `--draft --map --judge --framework-file --state` and **never sees the visual-set plan** — the plan is validated separately by `validate-visual-set.py`, and approved visuals reach the gate only *inside the draft text*. The join is real but it runs through the draft's content and the judge's `draft-sha256`, not through a shared argument. The second false binding this amendment shipped; see the `--verdicts` correction above.
>
> **What this buys, stated honestly because the issue states it honestly:** the win scales with claim count and issue-thread depth, compressing the examinations to the slowest single claim and the judging to the slowest shard. **It does not touch the largest observed sitting costs**, which are owner-paced gates and model composition time, and must not be sold as fixing those. **No new standing checkers**, per the owner's checker-growth ruling: verification is the existing reproduction-style tests extended to cover the derived ledger and `verify-provenance`'s existing test surface extended to the sharded union. **What would overturn this amendment:** a concurrent run producing a pin ledger that differs from its serial counterpart, which would mean the derivation is not order-independent after all and the answer becomes serializing the join rather than deriving it. Delivery: stories 20.162 (examine writer contracts and intra-examination concurrency), 20.163 (sharded attestation), 20.164 (fill-level fan-out and the visual-set/judging join).

> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1249) — the issues source's THREAD FETCHES move to the REST core pool with conditional requests; the term-searched LIST call stays where it is, because no core-pool equivalent of it exists.** The amendment above fans examinations out per claim, and the issues source is the network-bound leg of each one — so the concurrency lands on whichever pool that source reads through. The pools are separate and unevenly loaded, measured at triage: `core` 4993/5000, `graphql` 4711/5000. **But the issue's framing treats "the issues source's GitHub reads" as one thing and they are two**, which is what this decision turns on: per examination there is **one** `gh issue list --search` (`scripts/examine.py:256-260`) and **M** per-issue thread fetches (`:287-290`). Over N claims the fan-out produces N list calls and **N×M** thread fetches — only the second term grows with the work, and it is the one that moves.
>
> **The contract:** per-issue comment fetches are issued against `repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/{number}/comments` on the core pool, with `If-None-Match`/ETag values cached per issue thread so repeat consultations across claims and runs return 304 and cost nothing against the limit. The search-list call remains on GraphQL. **The population is unchanged, and that is the decisive property** rather than a convenient one: the search that SELECTS which issues an examination sees is untouched, so nothing moves in what the provenance map cites or what `verify-provenance` resolves against, and the examine output contract (evidence items, `cite` forms, `time_axis`) is preserved by construction rather than by care. The coverage rule is likewise untouched — *"an empty result from an unreachable source is a different finding from an empty result from a read source"* (`specs/spec-writing-assistant/amendments.md`, the 2026-08-02 #1182 amendment) — and an unreachable REST endpoint stays **cannot-determine**, never absence.
>
> **Two premises of #1249 are corrected rather than quietly dropped.** **(a)** It states the footprint is `examine.py`'s issues source *"and one call in `run-checks.sh`"*. `scripts/run-checks.sh` contains **no** `gh` call — both occurrences are comments (`:27`, `:87`, the latter being the phrase "before `gh pr create`") — and `scripts/examine.py` is the only file under `scripts/` that invokes `gh` at all. The footprint is one file. **(b)** It states that *"the equivalent `gh api repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues...` calls ride the REST core pool"*. That holds for endpoints which are not search, and the current list call **is** term-searched; the REST endpoint preserving its semantics is `/search/issues`, whose pool is **limit 30**, verified at triage beside the two 5000s above. Under concurrent examinations that pool exhausts faster than the one being fled, so the literal migration the issue proposes is refused on its own measurement.
>
> **The full-REST alternative is declined and its trigger is named, so it is not reopened as an oversight.** Enumerating `repos/{}/issues --paginate` and filtering terms locally would put everything on core and would additionally retire the OR-matching term derivation this repository's own governing text already calls *"known-noisy"* — a real gain. It is declined because it **changes the population**: server-side search ranking capped by `--limit` is not the same set as enumerate-then-filter, and a population change silently rewrites what a provenance map cites. It also costs roughly thirteen paginated calls per repository at this repository's issue count, which moves the load-bearing work to the cache rather than to the pool choice. **What would overturn this decision:** the GraphQL pool becoming the binding constraint on a real draft — N list calls at observed claim counts approaching the limit — at which point the full-REST option is the escalation, taken deliberately with a re-baselined population rather than as a patch.
>
> **Ordering, and the two kinds are kept apart.** 20.165 and story 20.162 both edit `scripts/examine.py`: that is a **file-overlap scheduling edge**, never a `depends_on` and never a dependency link. Separately and genuinely, 20.165 should merge **before** story 20.164, because 20.164's per-claim fan-out is what multiplies the calls this story moves — which is #1249's own argument for its timing. Delivery: story 20.165.

> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1287) — a PROVENANCE VERDICT IS KEYED BY (position, text hash) AND CARRIED ACROSS EVERY JUDGE ROUND OF THE RUN; a position whose text is unchanged is never re-graded, pass or fail, and a disagreement on unchanged text is a SURFACED DEFECT rather than absorbed cost.** Run `20260802T185710-622820` spawned **nine** isolated provenance judge rounds before ever passing the quality gate, and nothing refused: both bounding mechanisms sit where that path never went. `TWO_CYCLE_BOUND` (`scripts/draft-pipeline.py:218`) is enforced at `quality-gate --cycle` and at `repair-hop` (`:1038`) — i.e. **after** a gate verdict — while the fill's judge-respawn loop runs **before** the first gate pass, where every draft edit legitimately moves the attested hash and so legitimately spawns a fresh judge. The same run's judge **oscillated**: position P11 passed one round and failed the next on **identical text**.
>
> **This spec already carries the anti-oscillation rule; it was written for one judge and never reached its sibling.** CAP-7 holds that the second cycle is *"a **bounded delta re-check** that cannot introduce a new interpretive dim1–2 judge finding (#349), so the two cycles converge rather than oscillate"* — and the Constraints section restates it. The 2026-07-26 (#738) amendment above extended #349's **delta scope** to `verify-provenance` (*"the provenance judge re-grades only sentences changed or added relative to the attested draft … carrying forward verdicts for untouched sentences"*), but not its **convergence** half. What is decided here is the application of that settled rule to the sibling judge, not a new principle.
>
> **The premise is corrected against the shipped code rather than assumed, because partial machinery exists and its three gaps are what the observed run walked through.** `delta_basis` (`scripts/verify-provenance.py:350-402`, read 2026-08-02) does carry forward sentence TEXTS that passed the attested prior cycle. (a) It is **caller-supplied and opt-in** — `--prior-worklist` / `--prior-verdicts` (`:464-474`), consumed only when both are passed alongside `--draft` (`:503`) — and the fill's pre-gate respawn loop passes neither, so the absence of a basis reads as an ordinary full re-grade and discloses nothing; the disclosed-fallback promise (`delta re-grade unavailable — full re-grade: <reason>`) fires only for a basis that was **offered** and failed to resolve. (b) The basis is the **single immediately-prior cycle**, never the run, so nine rounds have no shared ledger even when each is passed the last one. (c) It carries **passes only** (`:399-401`): a position that FAILED and was not edited is re-graded and may flip — which is exactly the observed P11 behaviour, **licensed** by the mechanism rather than caught by it.
>
> **The rule.** A provenance verdict is keyed by **(position, sha256 of that position's segmented sentence text)** — the segmentation is already mechanical and tool-owned per the 2026-07-26 (#755) amendment above, so the key rests on the one segmentation authority and not on a re-derived quantity. The keyed verdict is **carried for the whole run**, across every judge round including the pre-gate fill rounds, and a position whose keyed text is unchanged is **never re-graded**. **Carrying a FAIL is the load-bearing half**, not a symmetry: an unedited failing position is still failing, and re-asking a judge about it is how a bound becomes a coin flip. When a verdict arrives for a key the ledger already holds and **disagrees** with it, the carried verdict stands and the disagreement is **disclosed by name** — fail-closed, on the same reasoning the 2026-08-02 (#1248) amendment above refuses concatenated attestations rather than taking last-wins. Isolation (NFR13) is unchanged and is what makes the carry safe: the judge receives a **smaller worklist** plus the `carried:` header it echoes, never prior verdicts as context.
>
> **Stated limit, so this is not read as more than it is: it does NOT cap total judge rounds. It bounds WASTE, never COST.** Nine rounds each grading text that genuinely changed would still run under this rule, and that is the intended behaviour — the defect is re-paying for a verdict already held, not the number of rounds a hard draft needs.
>
> **The cost half was considered and NOT selected, and its dependency is recorded so the decline is not read as an oversight.** Relocating `budget-check` (`scripts/draft-pipeline.py:2680`, thresholds `:2677`, `judge_rounds: 12`) into the fill's judging loop so rounds are compared **mid-fill** rather than only at stage boundaries would bound cost — but its basis is `run-events.jsonl` `judge-round` events, which the observed run did not emit (`_cost_proxies`, `:2629-2650`, fell back to counting verdict artifact files), and per-block emission is #1286's work. **Open question, not a blocking premise:** whether an in-fill budget check is needed **once** judge rounds are mechanically observable is left open and tracked at **#1286**; nothing here depends on that answer, and this amendment lands without it.
>
> **What would overturn this:** a position whose sentence text is unchanged while its **map row** changed — a re-classification from `derived` to `sourced`, or an edited pointer set — since the carried verdict would then be answering a question the map no longer asks. The key is under-specified for that case, and the correction is to widen it to the map row rather than to abandon the carry. Delivery: story 20.170.

> **Amended 2026-08-02 (triage, #1288) — the per-section evidence-type check RE-ANCHORS on the examination pin ledger, and its unrunnable case becomes a NAMED STATE rather than an omitted line.** The check (added 2026-07-19, #413/#414) resolved KINDs out of the run state's `fact_sheet`. That producer was retired with harvest (#1182/#1224), so on run `20260802T185710-622820` the strict invocation refused at `scripts/draft_variants.py:273-282` — *"--state carries no fact sheet … refuses rather than reporting a false evidence gap"* — and the gate was then run **without** `--framework-file`/`--state`, which drops the check entirely and reports nothing about the drop. **The refusal is not the defect; the route around it is.** Story 19.14 (#751) built that refusal deliberately, so that "computed over nothing" could never present as "computed and found nothing"; what #1182 removed was the *only* input that could satisfy it, converting a fail-closed check into a **silent skip** — a gate that looks complete while one of its checks is structurally unrunnable. That is the #933 vacuous-pass shape exactly, against a rule this engine already carries (`specs/spec-writing-assistant/SPEC.md:81` clause (b)).
>
> **The re-anchor, and why it is a re-pointing rather than a new store.** The post-harvest evidence carrier is `$WS/examination-pins.txt`, DERIVED from the examination records in claim order (`scripts/examine.py:607-635`), read beside the provenance map's per-section sourced/derived distribution (`parse_provenance_map`, `scripts/draft-pipeline.py:258`). The pipeline **already treats that file as the fact sheet's successor**: stage 3 passes it as `--fact-sheet` to `verify-provenance` (`skills/draft-article/stages/stage3.md:320`), and the examine step records that the flag name was retained on purpose — *"the flag name predates #1182; the file it takes is the pin ledger"* (`skills/draft-article/stages/examine.md:121-122`). This amendment points **one more consumer** at a carrier that already exists; it creates no store, and it changes nothing about what the run writes.
>
> **The KIND mapping is PARTIAL, and the open half is carried rather than invented.** A fact-sheet entry was `CLAIM / SOURCE / KIND` over `event | quote | result | number`, and `EVIDENCE_KINDS` maps the declared types onto it — `episode ← {event}`, `example ← {event, quote, result}`, `measurement ← {number, result}` (`scripts/draft-pipeline.py:846-850`). **A pin ledger line is a bare pointer with no kind field** (`scripts/examine.py:629-632`), and the record it derives from types evidence as `source_type` (`commit | issue | prose`) plus `time_axis`, which is an orthogonal axis — not a renaming of the four KINDs. **What is established:** `episode` maps onto the **time-axis** predicate `verify-provenance` already enforces per claim (`scripts/verify-provenance.py:405-435`; the pointer→source-type table is `scripts/resolve-writing-sources.py:598`), and examine itself already declares the negative case at the source — a prose item carries `admissible_for: "state claims only — not an episode source"` (`scripts/examine.py:495-499`). `none` is trivially satisfied. **What is NOT established:** `example` and `measurement` have no predicate over the pin ledger, because nothing in the post-harvest record distinguishes a quote from a result from a number. **That is recorded as an open question, not resolved here** — inventing a mapping would put a fabricated predicate under a *publish blocker*, which is a worse failure than the one being fixed.
>
> **Which is why the three-valued clause binds FIRST and independently.** Whatever the re-anchor reaches, the gate reports the check's state as its own line — `evidence-type check: cannot-determine — <reason>`, naming the section and the declared type — never silence and never a pass. On the open half above, that line is the *correct* output and not a stopgap: a declared `example` minimum with no resolvable predicate is genuinely cannot-determine, and saying so is what keeps the gate's completeness claim true. **What would overturn this amendment:** the examination record gaining a per-evidence kind field that reproduces the quote/result/number distinction, at which point `example` and `measurement` become mechanical and the cannot-determine line stops firing for them; or a ruling that the type declaration is dead with harvest, at which point the check is retired explicitly (issue #1288's option 2) rather than left reporting cannot-determine forever. The mandated ask is one ask, and the owner answers the audience exactly once per run.

> **The accepted cost, stated plainly rather than glossed.** The mandated tier grows from two members to three. The tier is deliberately meant to stay small: its boundedness is the argument that the ≤10-minute owner-attention budget survives it — "the tier is bounded by construction (at most the reconciliation gate + the depth offer), so the ≤10-minute owner-attention budget still holds" (`scripts/draft-pipeline.py:2123-2125`). Three is still bounded by construction, but the growth is real and is the price of this decision: every fresh run pays one more owner-paced question, and that comment's parenthetical is now an enumeration of three. A **fourth** member must not be added without re-deriving the attention budget rather than asserting it.
>
> **The counter-argument the decision was taken against, and what is deliberately NOT invented here.** If the owner's audience is stable across runs, a per-run mandated ask is friction, and a declared default that pre-fills or skips the ask would be the better shape. The declared-default machinery **is shipped**: `scripts/resolved_defaults.py` (Story 20.62, #945) with the plan field `resolved_defaults` (`scripts/write-article-plan.py:206`, parser `:365`) under CAP-3's single-axis clause — a one-axis choice resolves by a **declared** default, visible and **overridable in the plan** rather than merely logged, and an undeclared default is not a default. Audience is single-axis over a closed vocabulary, so it is classifiable by that resolver, whose own fixture uses `audience` as the worked example (`scripts/check-resolved-default-inner.sh:81`). Two things are nonetheless **absent, verified rather than assumed**: no shipped configuration key declares an audience default (`declared_in` is a caller-supplied pointer, and neither `scripts/resolve-writing-sources.py` nor `scripts/validate-config.py` carries an audience field), and nothing wires the resolver into `cmd_interview` — its only consumer is the plan validation at `scripts/draft-pipeline.py:3403`. The skip precedent exists only on the depth side (`_state_depth`, `:2149-2163`), with no audience counterpart in run state. **Open question, left open rather than invented:** where a declared audience default lives, and whether it pre-fills the ask (visible and overridable, per CAP-3) or skips it (per `_state_depth`).
