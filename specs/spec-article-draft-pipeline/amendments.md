> **Amended 2026-07-26 (triage, #738)** per /triage-gh on the judge-loop cost profile (run 20260726T165310: 13 judge subagents across ~6 revision rounds, ~229k output tokens — the run's single largest multiplier; per-round full sentence-by-sentence provenance re-grades of 30–38k tokens each).
